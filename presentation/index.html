The Problem

India's Investors Are Underserved

🔒
Information Asymmetry
Institutional investors have Bloomberg terminals, quant teams & algo infra. Retail investors get static charts and news headlines.
🌐
English-Only Platforms
90% of Indians prefer their regional language. Zero trading platforms offer NLP-based analysis in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.
⚙️
Algo Trading Gap
Algo trading is 70% of US volume but only 5% of Indian retail. Existing tools are too technical and code-heavy for mass adoption.
📊
Complexity Kills Adoption
Candlestick charts, RSI, MACD — new investors don't understand technical analysis. They need plain-language guidance.
💸
Expensive Advisory
SEBI-registered advisors charge ₹10,000–₹50,000/year. Most new investors in Tier 2/3 cities can't afford professional advice.
🧠
Emotional Trading
80% of retail traders lose money due to emotional decisions. They need AI-driven, data-backed confidence — not panic selling.

Risk Analysis

What Could Go Wrong & How We Fix It

⚡ Execution Risk
Threat: Not iterating fast enough, losing momentum against well-funded incumbents.
Mitigation: Relentless execution cadence. Ship weekly. Stay lean and founder-led. Hard work is the only answer.
📜 Regulatory Risk
Threat: SEBI tightens algo trading rules or bans retail algo APIs.
Mitigation: Compliance-first approach from Day 1. Partner with SEBI-registered brokers. Stay within sandbox guidelines.
🏢 Big Player Entry
Threat: Zerodha or Groww launches their own AI chatbot feature.
Mitigation: Speed of innovation. NLP in regional languages + community repo is hard to replicate quickly. First-mover data advantage.
🤖 AI Hallucination
Threat: AI gives incorrect stock advice leading to user losses and trust erosion.
Mitigation: Ground AI in real data (20+ indicators, live prices). Mandatory disclaimers. RAG pipeline with verified data sources.
📉 Market Downturn
Threat: Bear market reduces retail trading volumes and user interest.
Mitigation: Expand to mutual funds, FDs, and gold investments — short & intermediate term products beyond equities.
💰 Funding Gap
Threat: Unable to raise capital before runway runs out.
Mitigation: Bootstrap-friendly model. Low infra costs (Vercel + Neon). Revenue from Day 1 via brokerage commissions.
